About

I like the unglamorous parts of software.

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at the University of Waterloo. The problems I enjoy most are the ones where "it works on my machine" isn't enough: two buyers racing for the last unit, a worker crashing between a database commit and an acknowledgement, a model that looks great until you check for future-data leakage. I build things, measure them honestly, and write down what I learned, including when the first benchmark measured the wrong thing.

Outside of code I'm a powerlifter, which is mostly the same discipline: show up, track the numbers, and don't skip the boring sets.

A flash-sale checkout engine that never oversells.

Ruby on RailsRedis LuaSidekiqPostgreSQLk6Docker
